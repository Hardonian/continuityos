# Canadian Sovereign Critical Minerals Resilience & Defense Framework

## 1. Executive Summary

Canada holds significant domestic reserves of the world's most critical defense minerals. However, supply chain continuity is exposed to processing concentration, single-corridor rail freight bottlenecks, and adversary export embargoes.

Aegis Continuity models **Canada's 31 Critical Minerals**, evaluating burn rates, refining dependencies, and strategic substitutions for NATO defense primes.

## 3. Defense Production Act (DPA) Strategic Option Trees

In the event of adversary export embargoes or maritime chokepoint interdictions, Aegis Continuity calculates bounded mitigation paths:
1. **Strategic Reserve Allocation**: Mandates direct quota distribution to Tier-1 defense primes.
2. **Polar Multi-Modal Freight Bypass**: Shifts transport from southern rail mainlines to the Arctic Port of Churchill and CCG Icebreaker escorts.
3. **Strategic Substitution**: Evaluates performance trade-offs (e.g., LFP cold-weather degradation, torque penalties).
